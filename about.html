Who we are

Innovative Testing Solution is a trading establishment in Al Khobar, supplying chemicals and laboratory solutions across Saudi Arabia.

We supply industrial and laboratory chemicals, laboratory equipment and instruments, and educational lab products to laboratories, educational institutions, industrial customers and commercial businesses.

Our work is simple to describe and hard to do well: get the right grade, in the right pack, to the right site, with the right paperwork. We stay responsive through the whole order, and we build long-term relationships with the teams we supply.

Our aim is to be the supply partner labs across Saudi Arabia trust first, supported by our branch in Dubai for customers in the UAE.

Questions

Good to know.

Do you show prices online?

No. Prices depend on grade, pack size, quantity and delivery location, so every enquiry gets a written quotation. Add items to your enquiry list or send us your own list.

Where do you deliver?

Across Saudi Arabia from our Al Khobar base, and across the UAE through our Dubai branch in Deira.

Can you supply items that aren't in the catalogue?

Yes. The online catalogue is a selection. Customised and requirement-based sourcing is a core part of what we do: send the item name, brand, part number or a photo.

Do chemicals come with documentation?

Chemicals are supplied with the manufacturer's Certificate of Analysis and Safety Data Sheet. Ask for specific documents in your enquiry.

Do you supply schools and universities?

Yes. We put together teaching-lab chemicals, glassware and kits for educational institutions, and can pack orders per class or per lab.
